# Ruskin

Everything the literary press reviewed in the last ten years, scored out of ten against one reader's taste profile.

Nineteen publications go in: Publishers Weekly, Book Marks, the NYRB, the LRB, LARB, the New Yorker, the Guardian, the NYT Book Review, the Atlantic, the Paris Review, the Chicago and Cleveland Reviews of Books, the LA Times, Full Stop, Electric Literature, The Millions, Kirkus, Bookforum and 4Columns.

**The unit is the book, not the review.** Three publications on the same novel is one row carrying three mentions, scored in a single pass over all three texts.

The feed does not filter on taste. Everything in the window is there, scored, whether it is close to the profile or nowhere near it. Anything at 7.0 or above is tagged recommended.

## What the score is

Seven weighted dimensions read out of review prose by keyword: historical causation, subject engine, formal ambition, prose register, tonal signature, scale, and independence of the press. Every dimension shows the terms it fired on, which is what makes a wrong score visible as a wrong score. Treat a number as triage, not as a reading of the book.

Nothing here is generated by a model. The descriptions are the publications' own standfirsts.

## Your ordering

The words are shared. What they are worth is not. The **Profile** screen re-scores any band, re-weights any dimension, switches off any rule or penalty, and the feed re-ranks in the browser with no rebuild.

## Privacy

Saving, passing and every preference stay in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. There is no analytics endpoint, no account, and no server beyond the static host.

## Where it comes from

This repository is the published build. The source, the crawler and the taste profile live elsewhere.
